Two calm minutes, guided by your own breathing

Calm Desk uses your webcam and the Presage SmartSpectra SDK to read your pulse, breathing and heart-rate variability while you follow a breathing guide.

Your camera

Turns on only when you press the button below, and turns off when the session ends or you stop it.

Your video

Calm Desk never records or saves video or images. The SmartSpectra SDK processes camera frames and contacts Presage's service to authenticate and meter usage; Presage's Telemetry and Privacy page describes exactly what is sent.

Your numbers

Readings stay in memory for this session only. Nothing is written to disk unless you choose "Save summary" at the end. Finishing discards everything.

Not medical

This is a relaxation aid, not a medical device. It can't diagnose anything or advise on your health.
